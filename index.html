How to play

  • 01
    Build your line

    Choose an item in the shop, then click an open tile. You can seal every entrance; enemies will breach barriers instead of getting stuck.

  • 02
    Build and improve

    Turrets fire automatically and can be destroyed. Blocks and traps can also take damage; destroyed structures return after a wave. Traps hit harder and can burn, poison, stun, or slow enemies. Drag across the grid or Ctrl-click to select multiple structures, then bulk upgrade or sell them. Upgrade the Command Core up to Level 50—advanced turrets unlock from Core Level 11.

  • 03
    Hold your ground

    Earn credits for every enemy defeated. Wave payouts grow from one wave to the next, and surviving repairs your base a little.

  • 04
    Control the battle

    Bosses arrive every 10th wave; super-mega bosses attack every 50 waves, and the Ultimate Last Boss arrives at wave 150. Clear the wave before a boss for a preparation bonus. Cycle speed up to 35×; the traveling Black Market offers 50×–100× boosts, exclusive defenses, and abilities for Light Tokens exchanged at 1 billion credits each. Adjust graphics for your device, unlock abilities, then activate them from the controls beneath the item list.
